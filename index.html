Xudong Xu     徐旭东

I am a researcher at Galbot, working on Embodied AI.

I recieved my Ph.D. degree from Multimedia Laboratory in the Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Dahua Lin. I recieved my B.E in Automation from Nanjing University in June 2018.

My research interests lie in creating interactive scenes for humanoid robotics, with a focus on humanoid navigation and loco-manipulation.
I am looking for highly motivated interns at Galbot. Please feel free to email me (xuxudong@galbot.com) if you are interested.
